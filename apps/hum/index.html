Hum

Hum plays the songs already on your phone — fast, no account, and a layout that adapts from phone to tablet. Browse by song, album, artist or playlist, and follow synced lyrics as the song plays.

What it does

Three things, done properly.

01

Lyrics that stay in sync

Look up synced lyrics for almost any song, or add your own. Hum highlights the current line, and a tap-to-sync editor times lyrics that aren't synced yet.

02

A real equalizer

Five bands, genre presets and a bass boost slider that apply to everything you play.

03

Everything you'd expect

Sleep timer, home screen widget, queue, favorites, search across your library, light and dark themes and Android Auto support.
