Fruit Slice

A fast, colourful fruit cutting game you play with one finger. Slice through a storm of fruit, chain cuts into combos, and whatever you do, don't touch the bombs.

Get it onGoogle Play
v1.9No adsWorks offline14 languages

What it does

Three things, done properly.

01

Three ways to play

Survival with three lives, a 60-second Rush against the clock, or a relaxed 90-second Breeze with no bombs. Pick Easy, Normal or Hard for each.

02

Slice with skill

Cut several fruit in one swipe for combo points, land critical hits, trigger frenzies, and watch out for frost, smoke and chili bombs.

03

Collect and come back

15 blades with their own perks, 11 hand-painted dojos, daily rewards, three new challenges every day and a Weekly Cup with medals.
