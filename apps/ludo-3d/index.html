Ludo 3D

Two Ludo games in one app, both on glowing neon 3D boards. Race four tokens home in Classic Ludo, or climb the ladders and dodge the snakes in Snake Ludo. Play the computer or pass the phone to friends.

What it does

Two games, one neon board.

01

Classic Ludo

Traditional four-token Ludo: roll a 6 to bring a token out, capture opponents, rest on safe stars and race into your home lane. Computer players come in Easy, Normal and Hard.

02

Snake Ludo

Snakes and ladders on a 100-square board with cartoon snakes and glowing ladders. Three difficulty levels, each with its own board.

03

Play it your way

Your own house rules, eight board themes, ten languages and phone or tablet layouts. Each game saves after every turn.

Privacy

Your game stays on your device. Not designed for children.

Settings, coins, themes and unfinished games are stored on your device. The app uses Google AdMob for ads, and Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to improve the game and fix crashes. Aimed at players aged 13 and over.
