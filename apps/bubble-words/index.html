Bubble Words

A playful spelling and vocabulary game for children aged 3 to 6. Your child sees a picture, then pops the floating letter bubbles in order to spell the word. Every word is read aloud.

What it does

Three things, done properly.

01

Over 1,800 everyday words

29 easy categories — animals, fruits, food, vehicles, the sea, the body, colours, numbers and more — each word with its own picture, short words first.

02

Made for little hands

Big, bright bubbles that are easy to tap, friendly sea-themed characters, gentle sounds and letters and words spoken aloud.

03

Safe for children

No ads, no accounts, no chat, no links out of the app, and a grown-ups-only question guards the Parent area.
