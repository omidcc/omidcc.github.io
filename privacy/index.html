Privacy

This website collects nothing.

tunerapps.net is a static site. It has no accounts, no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts or fonts. Everything loads from this domain.

Hosting logs

Like any website, the host (GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and requested URL, under its own privacy policy. We don't have access to them for tracking purposes.

Email

If you write to us, we keep your message and address only to reply and to follow up on what you asked about. We never sell or share them.
