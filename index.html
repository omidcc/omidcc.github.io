About us

The team that sweats the details.

tunerapps is a team of designers and developers. We do the design, the code, the release notes and the support emails ourselves — so nothing gets lost between departments, and every detail has an owner.

We build the apps we would want to use ourselves — a music player with synced lyrics, an arcade game and a spelling game for kids — and we keep tuning them long after launch.
